About FriendlyAI

FriendlyAI is an AI assistant designed to make everyday AI tools easy to use in one place. It is free to get started and available on the web, with install options for mobile devices.

What can FriendlyAI do?

FriendlyAI supports AI chat, image analysis and editing, voice input, photo comparison, image combining, and an image gallery for saving and reusing your work.

Languages

You can chat in English, Malay, and Brunei Malay.

Use FriendlyAI

Open FriendlyAI and start chatting or working with images.
