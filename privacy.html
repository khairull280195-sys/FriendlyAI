Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

FriendlyAI is an AI assistant available on the web and Android. This policy explains the information used when you use FriendlyAI.

Information you provide

FriendlyAI may process your account email, chat messages, images you upload, and voice input you choose to provide in order to deliver the app's features.

How information is used

Your information is used to authenticate your account, provide AI responses, save your chat history, process image features, maintain security, and operate the service.

Third-party services

FriendlyAI relies on service providers for authentication, hosting, data storage, AI processing, and related infrastructure. Information necessary to provide a requested feature may be processed by those providers.

Your choices

You choose what you send to FriendlyAI. Avoid submitting highly sensitive information that is not necessary for your request.

Security

FriendlyAI uses authentication and access controls intended to protect user data. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes

This policy may be updated as FriendlyAI changes. The latest version will be posted on this page.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, use the FriendlyAI developer/support contact provided with the service.
